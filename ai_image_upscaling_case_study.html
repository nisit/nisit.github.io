1. The Challenge

A Direct-to-Film (DTF) print business paid a third-party API to upscale customer artwork to print resolution. Off-the-shelf upscalers are built for photos. On logos and lettering they blur thin strokes, invent detail and leave visible fringes around the design, and on printed film those flaws show. The business wanted better print quality, lower cost per image, and control over the technology.

2. How I Approached It

The right model for print

Deployed Real-HAT-Sharper, a transformer-based super-resolution model whose sharp edge reconstruction suits lettering, logos and linework, the content that generic upscalers handle worst.

Print-aware engineering

Designed proprietary post-processing tuned to how DTF film actually prints, so output arrives print-ready with no manual clean-up.

Production-grade service

Packaged the work as a secured, API-first service on serverless GPUs. It scales to zero when idle, warms on demand, and plugs into the existing production system without code changes.

Measured optimization

Profiled every stage, validated each change on real production artwork, and calibrated GPU settings for speed without giving up output accuracy.
