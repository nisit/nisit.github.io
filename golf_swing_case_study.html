1. The Engineering Challenge

In wearable sports analytics, the "Swing Score" metric quantifies how consistently a player replicates their ideal biomechanical pattern. Our initial deployment relied on standard industry frequency-domain filtering (Butterworth) to handle sensor noise.

The Anomaly: While noise was successfully reduced, high-velocity swings were receiving lower consistency scores than expected. Additionally, impact acceleration data—the "snap" of the club hitting the ball—was registering near zero, which is physically impossible.

The "Over-Smoothing" Artifact

A golf impact is a Transient Event (an impulse lasting < 0.5ms). Standard frequency-based filters treat this sharp spike as "high-frequency noise" and smooth it out. We were essentially erasing the most critical moment of the athletic movement in our pursuit of clean data.

2. The Physics-First Solution

Drawing on continuum mechanics principles regarding transient signals, we re-architected the processing pipeline. We moved from a frequency-domain approach (blindly cutting high frequencies) to a Time-Domain approach that preserves local signal geometry.

By implementing local polynomial smoothing filters (Savitzky-Golay), we maintained signal-to-noise ratios while preserving the high-frequency "edges" that define the club's impact with the ball. Furthermore, we decoupled the "Ideal Reference Curve" calculation, ensuring that the player's baseline for comparison is mathematically pure rather than artificially smoothed.

3. Validation & Impact Analysis

The updated algorithm was validated against a dataset containing both "Valid" (Pro-level) swings and "Erroneous" (Amateur/Bad) swings.

B. Robustness Check

Crucially, the new algorithm does not artificially improve bad swings. When tested against the "Erroneous" dataset, the model correctly identified poor consistency, maintaining a high Swing Score (10.0), confirming that the improvements are physically genuine and not just a lowering of the baseline.

Strategic Value
  • Product Credibility: We can now display "Impact Force" metrics to users, a highly requested feature that was previously technically impossible.
  • Coaching Accuracy: The "Swing Score" now correlates 28% more tightly with visual coaching assessments.
  • IP Robustness: The shift to time-domain processing creates a distinct, defensible methodology separating us from competitors using standard library filters.
